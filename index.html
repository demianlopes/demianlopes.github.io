Security & compliance

SOC 2 Type I for a digital banking platform

Certified · Led end to end

The challenge

Banks and credit unions trusting us with their customers needed independent proof that our security, availability and confidentiality controls were real and repeatable, not just written down.

What I did

  • Owned the certification from scoping to audit
  • Wrote and rolled out the security policies and risk management framework
  • Assigned control owners and automated compliance workflows and evidence collection in Vanta
  • Set up continuous monitoring and a remediation process for vulnerabilities

The result

SOC 2 Type I certification, and a control framework the team now runs day to day, ready to carry into Type II.

AI governance

Governing AI in banking with ISO/IEC 42001

Certified · Led end to end

The challenge

Bringing AI into banking meant financial institutions needed assurance about privacy, data handling and how the AI would be governed over time.

What I did

  • Led the ISO/IEC 42001 certification from start to finish
  • Implemented the policies and controls of an AI management system
  • Built in clear accountability and risk-based review for AI features
  • Made responsible AI practices part of normal product delivery

The result

An ISO/IEC 42001-certified AI banking platform, giving institutions a recognized standard to point to when regulators and customers ask how AI is governed.
